# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities to security@emit-gmbh.de, not in a public issue. Include the affected product and
version, how to reproduce the problem and the impact you expect. A person reads every report: we confirm
receipt, keep you informed while we fix it and agree on a disclosure date with you.

## Disclosure

Once a fix is available, we publish a security advisory in CSAF format with the affected versions, the
impact, the severity and the remediation, and credit you unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

annotate runs in the local stacks and dev deployments of other projects, behind their basic auth. Reports about the
service, the client's handling of untrusted input and what it records, and the CLI are in scope.

## Supported versions

Each released version receives security fixes for 5 years after its release. Security
updates are free of charge and stay available for at least ten years after their release.
